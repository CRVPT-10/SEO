National Chung Cheng University

Meet Chung Cheng among green paths and big ideas.

A research university in Chiayi, Taiwan, where knowledge reaches beyond the classroom into the hills, community, and everyday observation.

A place to think

A campus can be a way of learning.

National Chung Cheng University sits in Minxiong Township, Chiayi County. Its generous scale, buildings, lawns, and seasonal scenery create a calm and focused atmosphere. For many students, CCU is more than a place to earn a degree; it is a journey toward forming an independent point of view. The humanities, social sciences, engineering, management, and education meet here, allowing a conversation to move from a class into a club, a laboratory, or an evening walk across campus.

The university values research and international exchange, while encouraging students to apply what they learn to real-world questions. You might prepare a report in the library, observe new technology at a research center, and then discuss a lecture with friends over dinner. This unhurried but energetic routine develops more than professional skills: it builds listening, collaboration, questioning, and problem-solving habits. For anyone curious about university life in Taiwan, CCU offers an entry point far from urban noise but full of ideas in motion.
